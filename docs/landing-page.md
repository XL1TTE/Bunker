## Section 1 — Brief Description

### Bunker — Survive by Persuasion, Not by Luck

Bunker is a social deduction party game for groups. A catastrophe has wiped out
the outside world, and your group has found one bunker — but it doesn't fit
everyone. Each player is dealt a hidden Character Sheet (profession, health,
luggage, a unique fact…) and must argue, reveal, and vote their way into a seat.
Convince the others you're worth keeping, or get left outside.

## Section 2 — AI-Generated Content for Every Game

### A Different Game Every Time, Powered by AI

Every game's content is generated fresh by an AI model themed to the host's
chosen card packs — so no two bunkers, professions, or player dilemmas are ever
the same.

**A Unique Bunker Every Game.** Each match opens with a freshly generated Bunker
Card: a catastrophe, a survival duration, and a free-prose description of what
the bunker has and lacks. The environment becomes the "usefulness lens" — the
same luggage can be priceless or worthless depending on the bunker you draw.

**Coherent Character Sheets.** One consolidated AI request builds the Bunker Card
and every player's seven-attribute Character Sheet at once, so the sheets are
thematically coherent with each other and with the bunker. You'll argue from a
character nobody has ever played before.

**Always Playable.** If the AI service is briefly unavailable, the game
transparently falls back to curated canned content so your group can keep
playing — you never get hard-blocked by an outage.

Identity stays private — the AI generates anonymous sheets by count; no player
identity is ever sent to the model.

## Section 3 — How to Play on Our Site

### Join or Host a Lobby

Browse public lobbies in the Lobby Browser and join with a code, or create your
own. The host picks card packs, sets capacity, and can fill empty slots with AI
bots that auto-ready.

### Get Ready and Start

Everyone toggles Ready. Once all human players are ready, the host starts the
game — the AI generates the Bunker Card and each player's secret Character Sheet
(expect a short "generating game…" wait).

### See the Bunker

Round 1 opens by revealing the Bunker Card to everyone and a 1-minute-per-player
intro where you introduce yourself.

### Reveal an Attribute

Each round, take turns revealing one attribute from your sheet (only in the
Reveal phase). Choose wisely — what you reveal shapes how others judge your value
to the bunker. On timeout, a random unrevealed attribute auto-opens so the game
never stalls.

### Discuss and Persuade

A 2-minute free-for-all chat, then 1 minute per player to make your closing case.
Use the discussion chat to argue why you belong and why someone else doesn't.

### Vote — Secret Ballot

Vote for who should be eliminated. Votes are secret — only a "player has voted"
signal shows during the phase; the full tally is revealed at elimination.

### Elimination and Tie-Break

The player with the most votes is eliminated. A tie triggers a second vote; a
second tie resolves with the Roulette — a cosmetic highlight that lands on the
player to eliminate.

### Survive or Lose

Rounds repeat until the remaining players fit the bunker's capacity. Those
inside are the Survivors and win; the rest are eliminated. Bots take their turns
automatically so the flow never stalls.

The game is automatic and turn-based — per-player and group timers move things
forward even if someone steps away.
